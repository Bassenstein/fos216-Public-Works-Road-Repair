03

How the Program Works

The tools form one annual loop. Condition data sets the plan, the plan sets the work, the work is recorded to a standard, and the record improves the next condition survey.

STEP 1

Assess

Rate every segment by PCR and group it into grades A–F by ward.

Study
STEP 2

Fund

Set the yearly budget, allocate it worst-to-best across the 15 wards, add casino funds.

Study
STEP 3

Repair

Log, prioritize, route and dispatch crews; close out each job with photos.

Tracker
STEP 4

Verify & Report

Verify completed work to the manual's standard and report by ward and method.

Manual + Tracker
Completed repairs update segment condition → next year's study starts from the new baseline.

Every grade band maps to one treatment. Cheap preventive work keeps good streets good; saw-cut reconstruction is the last resort.
